# Little Life

A playful, browser-based life simulator inspired by the life-simulation genre. Start a random or custom life, make choices, build relationships, develop skills, follow a career, and watch your story unfold. The browser implementation uses data and adapted gameplay from Life-Simulator1 and OpenLife, both MIT-licensed. See `third_party/README.md` for notices and provenance.

## How to play

- **Age up** to begin a new year. Life events depend on your age and circumstances, and eligible events are not repeated until the current pool has been exhausted. Event choices show their effects and must be resolved before continuing. Rare source-inspired events include lightning strikes and kidnapping.
- Make up to **three activities, relationship, career, or asset decisions** per year. Viewing menus is free, and event choices don't consume a moment. Your moments reset when you age up.
- Play with age-appropriate toys as a child or try arts and crafts; inspiration may fail, and in-game depression can reduce enjoyment. Choose a gender at the start of a new life, or leave it random. Each new life has a trait affecting activities or annual changes; repeated meditation and studying can unlock additional traits. Read age-appropriate books at the library, either quickly or page by page with saved progress, or choose a movie by genre (including age-gated adult titles); book lovers and nerds gain extra benefits.
- From age 5, study to improve grades or skip school at the cost of grades, smarts and karma. Starting elementary, middle and high school brings a small learning bonus. Drop out from age 16, return before adulthood, or earn a $500 GED later. A diploma is required for university.
- Practice music, art, and fitness from age 8. Music and fitness can unlock special careers. Apply for jobs and answer interview questions. Work hard to build performance: 80% performance at the start of a new year earns a promotion and a 15% raise (up to level 5). Named roles can advance through OpenLife promotion chains. By default, retire from a job at 65 or older to receive a pension worth 30% of your salary; taking another job replaces it. The optional career-based pension rule requires ten recorded years in the current job and uses the five highest salaries, with possible forced retirement after 70. Quitting a job or retiring requires confirmation; quitting resets records for the current-job pension. Old work history is not fabricated.
- At work, choose a schedule from 38 to 70 hours. Pay scales with hours; longer hours can improve performance but build stress; high stress hurts health and happiness. Meditation reduces stress. Request a raise once per year; repeated rejected requests can lead to dismissal. Low performance can also result in a warning and dismissal.
- Play the lottery from age 18: buy one or ten $4 tickets and match four distinct numbers from 1 to 20 to win the jackpot. Cosmetic surgery costs $25,000; surgeon reputation affects the chance of a potentially fatal complication, and there is a six-year cooldown.
- Friendship and partner bonds fall by 3 each year and can end if neglected. Spend time together to strengthen them. Marriage and children require a strong enough partner bond. New lives also have aging parents; visiting them raises their bond, which falls by 2 yearly. A bond below 40% halves a parent’s eventual inheritance. Parents, siblings, known-age children and partners with age profiles can die suddenly; a kidnapping loss can also result in a funeral. When a parent, sibling, child or profiled partner dies, choose whether to attend their funeral (or remember them privately during a prison year); multiple losses are handled one at a time and remain pending across reloads. Some new lives have siblings, who age and can be visited; profiled siblings have smarts, looks and petulance, which may reduce conversation agreement. Older saved siblings retain their previous behavior. Use More ways to connect to discuss topics, compliment, insult, give gifts, or ask a parent for money; kind or unkind choices affect karma. Insults require confirmation and a petulant sibling or volatile partner may retaliate. New parents have generosity and wealth ratings influencing requests. With newly generated parents, a gift or three refusals starts a three-year cooldown; earlier profiled parents retain their existing cooldown, and older unprofiled parents retain their bond-based rule. Meet a potential date and decide whether to ask them out, propose, choose a wedding venue, and renew vows. A spouse can decline a vow renewal when the bond is low; a rejected request can be retried the next year, while a successful renewal costs $500 and can be repeated after ten years. For newly profiled partners, proposal acceptance considers time together, bond and personality; after a rejected proposal, wait until next year to ask again. Calling off an engagement requires confirmation. The original direct-marriage shortcut remains available. Newly generated partners have a lifespan; their death does not incur a divorce settlement. Older partners without a lifespan retain their previous behavior. Divorce requires confirmation and costs 40–60% of your cash as a settlement. Trying for a baby can start a pregnancy that resolves the following year; ending it requires confirmation. With newly profiled partners, the decision also depends on time together, bond and a fictional conception chance; this is a game mechanic, not medical advice. Older partners keep the previous guaranteed rule.
- The adoption agency offers a randomly generated child aged 1–17 and a quoted fee of $10,000–$30,000 for players aged 21–70, including those with partners. Agency assessments become the child’s independent stats. Children may study, work, save, date and marry as they grow up. New child profiles have an individual relationship-interest rating that influences dating and can fall after a breakup; some newly recorded marriages can divorce while others remain together. Older child profiles keep their previous relationship rules. A child starting university now takes a $5,000 student loan ($2,500 with 85% smarts), repaid from their own savings after studies; old unrecorded loans are not invented. Newly generated child partners have independent profiles and lifespans; those profiles follow the child if you continue as them. A child’s recorded marriage year also follows them for vow-renewal timing; older marriages without a recorded date keep the previous behavior. If you have a partner, an agency adoption records them as the other adoptive parent; the existing single-parent adoption option remains available.
- Under More ways to connect, individual outings and conversations report enjoyment or agreement, and compliments report appreciation. Each rewards the first successful interaction of its type per person each year. Someone with a very low bond may refuse. An argument offers an apology, agreement to disagree, or an insult; the choice persists across reloads.
- From age 19, pay 20% income tax by default (or switch to Life-Simulator1’s progressive brackets in Assets) and $12,000 in annual living costs ($8,000 with a house), plus $2,000 per child under 18 and $500 for a pet. Unpaid living costs reduce happiness but do not create debt. University takes four years and costs $5,000, or $2,500 with at least 85% smarts. Pay up front, ask a parent, apply for a full scholarship once per year, or take a student loan; up to $1,000 is repaid yearly after studies end. While enrolled, study to improve grades, skip lectures at the cost of grades, smarts and karma, or leave; very low grades can lead to expulsion. Choose a major to unlock related careers; prior saved graduates retain access to all degree careers. Odd jobs can help pay for school without leaving it.
- In Assets or Milestones, view life statistics; Assets also offers a financial overview of net worth and estimated cash flow, deposit and withdraw savings in fixed or custom whole-dollar amounts, borrow or repay bank debt, and trade single or multiple units of funds, stocks, metals, and cryptocurrency. Savings earn 10% per year; bank debt grows by 20%, with up to 10% automatically repaid from cash each year. Both are paused in prison. Market prices may fall sharply. Cash, savings, bank debt, income and the original investment fund are each capped at $1 trillion; sales that would exceed the cash cap are disabled. Bankruptcy can clear bank debt but removes cash, savings, investments, possessions, and employment; student loans remain.
- Choose a diet for annual gameplay effects and costs. Get a driver’s, boating, or pilot license before buying vehicles; browse the property catalogue for more car models, boats, planes, and homes. Resale values begin at 70% of the purchase price; vehicles lose 15% of their value each year. Move to a new city or choose from the upstream country catalogue (locations do not change laws or currency), adopt as a single parent, or adopt a cat, dog, or fish. New pets have a breed and lifespan; pets from older saves remain compatible.
- Crime can earn money or lead to fines and prison. An arrest ends your job, studies, or pension, and convictions restrict certain careers. While in prison you can read, exercise, write to loved ones, or get medical care. Age up to serve your sentence; regular living costs are suspended for those years.
- Multiple illnesses can occur at once; choose which condition to treat. Colds and flu can resolve naturally; depression, loneliness and high blood pressure may also resolve under certain in-game conditions. Other illnesses can harm health until treated. Cancer complications and a rare heart attack during severe in-game high blood pressure can be recorded as causes of death. Anxiety, loneliness and depression can develop under certain in-game conditions; these mechanics are not medical advice. Chronic conditions may need multiple doctor visits. Medical care is covered for children under 18; adult treatment costs $500, while a regular checkup costs $100. Unproven alternative treatments carry a serious risk of harm. Very low happiness also hurts health.
- Collect milestones along the way and review your life when it ends, including cause of death where recorded, karma and a lifetime-happiness score based only on recorded years. Older lives without a recorded cause are marked as such. Milestones stay unlocked for that life even if circumstances change. At the end of a life, you can continue as a child whose birth age is known. Newer children keep their own stats, education, jobs, partner, savings, recorded student loans, work history and happiness as they grow up, and bring them when you continue as them. Recording starts when the feature is available; earlier years, including years before adoption, are not invented. Children without profiles in older saves start with new stats. By default they inherit cash, savings, bank debt, investments, and possessions (but not the parent’s student loans). A child retains their own recorded student loan. Children with known ages can die suddenly and profiled children can also die of old age. They are remembered under Family legacy and are no longer available for interactions or succession. A will naming a child who dies reverts to the playable heir; wills naming other children remain attached to them. A will can instead name a child or spouse, divide the estate among living children and spouse, or leave it to charity; the selected playable child receives their share. If a spouse is no longer your partner, the will reverts to the playable heir. If a child’s recorded co-parent is still the current partner and has a recorded lifespan, they remain a living parent in the next generation, with a new 65% bond and no recorded inheritance. Ex-partners, partners without a recorded lifespan and older profiles without a recorded co-parent are not reconstructed. Other relationships start fresh. The last five past lives remain readable under **Family legacy**. Choosing New life replaces the entire family archive. Older saves are preserved but don’t gain retroactive traits, siblings, or parents, and children with unknown birth ages cannot be selected.

## Saves and privacy

Progress automatically saves in the browser's local storage. No account or backend is required. Saves are local to the browser and device; clearing site data removes them. Use **Save & load** to keep up to ten separate local copies, or download a JSON backup and restore it on another device. Loading or deleting a local copy requires confirmation; copies share the browser’s storage limit and can also be removed by clearing site data. Restoring replaces the current life after confirmation. If browser storage is unavailable, the game displays a warning and a backup can still be downloaded. If an existing autosave cannot be read or validated, it is preserved rather than overwritten; the temporary life does not autosave until you explicitly replace the original. If accessing browser storage itself failed, use Save & load to retry reading the original before replacing it; once accessible, its original bytes can be downloaded or the page reloaded to try resuming it. Save & load also offers a download of unreadable autosave or slot data for recovery, and can remove unreadable slot data separately after confirmation.

## Run locally

Serve this directory with any static web server, for example:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`. No build step is needed. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when available, with system fallbacks when offline.

## Tests

With Node.js installed, run `npm install` followed by `npm test`. The test dependency is not needed to play or deploy the game.

## Deploy to Cloudflare Pages

Connect this repository to Cloudflare Pages and use **None** as the framework preset. Leave the build command empty and set the build output directory to `/` (the repository root). Deploy. The site consists entirely of static files, so it also works with Pages Direct Upload.
